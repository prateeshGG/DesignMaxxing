## 2. Already consistent (founder to confirm)

- **Table names:** `discovery_run`, `discovered_url`, `seed_import_row` (2.1) and `global_breaker` (2.9) follow the snake_case singular convention. 2.2 owns no table.
- **Who writes what:** 2.9 writes `capture_session` only through `openCaptureSession` and `closeCaptureSession`, and the `source.breaker_*` columns only through `recordBreakerTransition`. 2.1 writes `robots_*` only through `recordRobotsFetch` and units only through `createUnits`.
- **The capture child holds no credentials** (1.1 matrix, 1.4 assumption, 2.2 R2, 2.9 R3): the supervisor uploads and commits evidence in one `withLease` transaction (1.3 §4.3, 2.2 §4.6, 2.9 S12).
- **The ladder:** 1.4 chooses the rung; the child is stateless (2.2 R3, 2.9 C2). Attempt 90 s, heartbeat 10 s, lease 60 s are the same in 1.4, 2.2 and 2.9.
- **Breaker numbers:** window 20, trip at 50%, open 6 h doubling to 7 d, one probe, state stored on `source` (1.5, 2.9, doc 22 §9). Same events in 1.4's catalogue.
- **Robots skips are `SKIPPED`, never `BLOCKED`** (1.5 R-4, 2.1 R-5, 2.2 R9), so they stay out of `BLOCKED %`.
- **Honest identity:** one user-agent from `getEffectivePolicy`, never per source, no evasion (1.5 R-5, 2.1 R-6, 2.2 R10, 2.9 R16).
- **Device profiles:** 2.2's values (`desktop` 1440×900 @2, `mobile` 390×844 @3) equal 1.2's seed rows.
- **Disk watermarks 80% / 90%** are the same in 1.1 E14, 1.3 E4 and 2.9 §3.7.
- **Policy is checked late:** at crawl-run creation (2.5), at each lease (1.4) and at publish (2.10); 2.1 re-checks before committing its plan.
- **F-45 and F-46** are applied in 2.1, 2.2 and 2.9 (crawler machine, person-chosen sites), and now in 1.1 and 1.3.
- **One reason-code list** (1.4 §3.11) and **one flag list** (1.4 §3.6) now cover 1.4, 2.1, 2.2 and 2.9.

## 5. Founder decisions from wave 2

1. **Run the crawler only when you press start.** It would run on your own computer only during a crawl session that you start and stop, never at boot. It never turns off, sleeps or restarts your computer, and while a page is being captured it asks the computer to stay awake. *Suggested answer: yes.* (OQ-2.9-35, OQ-2.9-27, OQ-2.9-37)
2. **If your computer sleeps or switches off in the middle of a page, should that count as a failed try?** Each page gets a few tries, each with a simpler method. A sleeping computer is not the website's fault. *Suggested answer: no. The try is not counted and the page is tried again the same way.* (OQ-1.4-25, OQ-2.9-37)
3. **Before launch, keep everything on your own computer?** That means the website, the database and the saved screenshots (on your disk instead of online storage), moving online only when the library is shown to users. Once online storage is used, a dropped internet connection is handled by saving captures in a waiting folder and uploading them later. *Suggested answer: yes.* (1.3 note, OQ-1.1-10, OQ-2.9-40)
4. **How much of your computer may the crawler use?** *Suggested answer: two browser windows at a time, running at low priority, pausing while your memory is tight or you are on battery; you can change these limits, and the first 20-site test will measure the real load.* (OQ-2.9-5, OQ-2.9-36)
5. **Should the 30-minute limit per site stop counting while you have paused the crawl?** *Suggested answer: yes. The clock counts only the time the crawler is really running, so closing the lid does not cost a site its remaining pages.* (OQ-1.4-26, OQ-2.9-38)
6. **How the crawler introduces itself.** It names our project and gives a contact link, the same for every site; it always uses English and UTC time; phone captures behave like a phone. Is it acceptable that this counts as a declared setup and not as a disguise? *Suggested answer: yes.* (OQ-2.2-8, OQ-2.2-10)

The six wave-1 decisions in [review-wave-1.md](review-wave-1.md) §5 still stand, except that wave-1 item 1 ("the Crawler VM") now reads "the crawler machine, your own computer" (F-45).
