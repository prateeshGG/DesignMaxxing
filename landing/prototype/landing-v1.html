Questions

What is DesignMaxxing?

A searchable library of real website designs, cut into sections so you can find the exact reference you need.

Which sites come first?

Public marketing websites, starting with software and developer products. Apps and flows come later.

Where do the screenshots come from?

We capture them ourselves with automated browsers. We follow robots.txt, limit our request rate and link each capture to its source.

What will it cost?

Pricing is not decided yet. Waitlist members will hear first and can tell us what feels fair.

When can I get in?

Early access opens in small batches. Join the waitlist and we will email you when it is your turn.
