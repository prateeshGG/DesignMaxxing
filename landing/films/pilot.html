Quality pilot · film 1 of 5 · 18.5 s · 1920×1200

How it works, rebuilt: sharper sources, real fonts, a moving camera.

Why the first versions looked cheap

  • The fonts never loaded in the renderer (the sandbox proxy broke Google Fonts), so every frame used fallback system fonts.
  • The screenshots were 1280 px crops of your 1920 px screenshots; zooming in made them soft.
  • Frames were rendered at 1× and squeezed into a 1280 px video at a low bitrate.
  • The motion was simple fades and slides with a static camera.

What changed in this pilot

  • Local font files; fresh 2× captures of the three sites (2880 px wide, phones at 3×).
  • Rendered at 2× and exported at 1920×1200.
  • A virtual camera: push-ins on details, an expo pull-back, a whip pan with motion blur, a match cut into the opened section, then a camera tour of the hotspots.
  • Springs with overshoot, masked text reveals, light sweeps on cards, counters that tick.

If this is the right bar, the other four films get the same treatment next.
